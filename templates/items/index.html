How Belong Works

Three simple steps to return lost belongings across campus.

1

Report

Publish a lost report or found listing with the campus building, date, and private verification details.

2

Connect

Claimants submit ownership proof. The finder or campus safety officer reviews evidence securely.

3

Recover

Meet in person and verify the handover with a time-limited 6-digit cryptographic OTP to confirm return.
